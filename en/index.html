I design analytics solutions that reach the decision.

I investigate the problem before answering it, design the method, bring in the people who need to build it, and follow up on whether the solution was actually used. I came to data from architecture and eight years of scientific research, and I use both.

The cycle that repeats across my projects. The dashed arrow is the part I care about most: going from application back to a better question.

Three ways of looking at a problem

They are not stages of my career. They are lenses I use at the same time.

Researcher

I question assumptions and separate what the evidence supports from what remains uncertain. I review the method before trusting the result.

26 scientific papers; a review of an impact evaluation that informed a leadership decision.

Architect

I read the context, what already exists and the constraints before designing. A solution has to fit the system it will live in.

Geoprocessing to measure business exposure to the 2024 floods in Rio Grande do Sul.

Solution builder

I turn knowledge into methods, models and tools that other people can apply and improve without me.

A governance framework applied by colleagues in other areas; a portfolio model built into routine management.

Case studies

Told with what worked, what didn't and what is still open. Program names, vendors and amounts are omitted.

  • Impact evaluation, Statistics, Executive communication

    Reviewing an impact evaluation before acting on it

    Asked to validate a vendor's report, I found methodological inconsistencies and, instead of only flagging them, built an alternative analysis with the program team that leadership could actually use.

    Read the case study

  • Geoprocessing, Data integration, Crisis

    Business exposure to the 2024 floods in southern Brazil

    Geocoded businesses crossed with satellite-mapped flood and landslide areas, to guide where support went first during the crisis.

  • Data governance, Method

    Data governance that starts from decisions

    A framework that starts from business questions and works back to sources and the views each decision needs. I applied it in the first area; colleagues applied and improved it in the next ones.

  • Modeling, Automation, Portfolio management

    Scoring the analytics solution portfolio

    A model that ranks solutions by usage and recency and recommends what to migrate, keep or review, built into the management process.
